Tic-Tac-Toe Study

Welcome!

You are invited to participate in a short study about how people notice and interact with notifications while completing a task.

During the study, you will play Tic-Tac-Toe while notifications appear on the screen. When a notification appears, interact with it as you normally would.

What to Expect

  • The study takes approximately 5–10 minutes.
  • You will play several rounds of Tic-Tac-Toe.
  • Notifications will appear while you play.
  • Your interactions with the notifications will be recorded.

Thank you for participating!

This study investigated what makes smartphone notifications noticeable while people are engaged in another task.

During the study, notifications varied in three ways:

  • Visual prominence — how visually noticeable the notification was.
  • Animation — how the notification appeared on screen.
  • Duration — how long the notification remained visible.

We recorded how you interacted with each notification, including whether you dismissed or opened it and how quickly you responded.

The purpose of collecting these responses is to better understand how notification design influences noticeability during everyday activities.

Your responses will be analyzed together with those of other participants. Your participant ID was randomly generated and was used to associate your responses across the study.
